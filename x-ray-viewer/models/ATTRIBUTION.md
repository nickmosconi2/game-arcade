# Anatomy model

BodyParts3D 4.0, © The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS), licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Mitsuhashi et al. (2009), BodyParts3D: 3D structure database for anatomical concepts,
Nucleic Acids Research, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkn613

Taken from the browser-ready packaging in github.com/ashemag/human-atlas (CC BY 4.0 notice there).
Adaptations for this app: the left and right arm structures (bones, muscles, arteries, veins)
were extracted, converted to millimetres, given 21 hand-joint landmarks, and each vertex was bound
to the forearm, palm or one of 15 finger bones so the model can be posed from hand tracking.

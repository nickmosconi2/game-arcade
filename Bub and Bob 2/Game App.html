      Bub & Bob 2

      Version 1.0 · Nicholas Mosconi

      Two dinos, 120 flooded rounds, and a lot of bubbles.

      Blow a bubble at a critter to trap it (it stays trapped for about 11 seconds), then touch the bubble to pop it. The critter turns into a treat; grab it for points. Wait too long and it breaks free, angrier and faster.

      Land on a bubble and press Jump right as you touch it (or hold Jump) to spring off its top and reach high ledges. Stand on a bubble too long and it pops under you. Fall through a gap in the floor and you drop in from the top.

      Hold the blow button to keep blowing bubbles at the normal pace. Up to 10 empty bubbles can be on screen at once (15 with bubble gum). Blow another and the oldest one pops to make room.

      Online play

      Choose Game › Play Online…, or click a name in the Arcade. Everyone who's been in the Arcade is on your Players list. Anyone sitting on a game's menu can be joined with one click: their game opens for you, no codes and no hosting first. As soon as a friend joins, a 2-player game starts.

      Whoever's game it is plays Bub, using either set of keys. The friend who joins plays Bob, using the arrow keys or W A D, Z or Up to jump, and X, Space or F to blow bubbles. Bob moves the instant you press a key, even online.

      Either player can pause. The host's computer runs the game, so the host never has any delay. The friend who joins sees their own dino move instantly too; everything else on their screen is a split second behind, depending on the distance between you.

      Chain pops

      Bubbles touching each other pop together. Each trapped critter in one chain is worth double the last: 1,000, 2,000, 4,000, then 8,000. Bigger chains also leave better fruit.

      EXTEND letters

      From round 5, letter bubbles drift in through the floor or the ceiling at random times, any letter in any order. Pop them to collect . Your letters light up down the side wall. Letter bubbles still floating pop once the last critter is gone, so grab them during the round. Spell EXTEND to earn an extra dino: a fanfare plays for 5 seconds and the round ends by itself. A letter you already have is worth 500 points.

      Water and lightning bubbles

      From round 5, one of these drifts in now and then (in about 1 round in 3).

      • Water bubble

        Pop it and a flood pours out. It grabs your dino and any critters it touches (your dino picks up any treats it passes on the way), runs down through the level (falling off ledges and turning back at walls) and drains off the bottom of the board, through the hole in the floor or, if it gets boxed in, straight down through the platforms. Everyone comes back out of the top of the screen, and every critter it carried turns into a diamond: 2,000 points for the first, doubling for each one after.

      • Fire bubble

        Pop it and a fireball drops straight down. Where it lands, the platform bursts into flames for about 5 seconds, and any critter that walks into the fire is knocked out: 1,000 points for the first, doubling for each one after. The fire doesn't hurt your dino.

      • Lightning bubble

        Pop it and a lightning bolt shoots out the opposite side from where you touched it, straight across the screen. Every critter in its path is knocked out and drops fruit, worth 1,000 points for the first and doubling up to 8,000.

      Critters

      Critters keep working their way down to the bottom of the stage, then drop through the hole in the floor and fall back in from the top. They only climb up when a dino is close by and above them.

      • PrickleA wind-up that walks and hops, and only turns round when it bumps into a wall. The only critter in rounds 1-4
      • GhostFrom round 5. White ghost. Walks the platforms; if it spots you on its level it stops, flashes !, then shoots fire
      • WobblerFrom round 11. Purple floater. Drifts around, doesn't shoot
      • ClankFrom round 21. Wind-up spring, jumps a lot
      • SkelCan't be trapped. Shows up if you dawdle
      • FlutterFrom round 41. Flies in waves through walls
      • RockerFrom round 31. Throws rocks that freeze you for 2 seconds
      • DiverFrom round 51. Marches and steps down
      • BlazeFrom round 61. Breathes fireballs

      Wobblers float: they can sink through the hole in the floor and rise through the hole in the ceiling. A ghost only fires when it's facing you on the same level with nothing in between, so ducking behind a platform keeps you safe. Rocks and fireballs knock you out just like touching a critter.

      Bubbles rise until a platform blocks them, slide along underneath it to the nearest gap and keep going up, then drift slowly along the ceiling to one gathering spot. That makes big chain pops easier to set up. A trapped critter's bubble turns orange as it weakens, then flashes red just before the critter breaks out.

      Treats

      Each popped critter turns into fruit. The more critters you pop in one chain, the better the fruit: the first is a banana, the second an orange, and so on. Each one is worth double the one before.

      • Banana1st · 500 pts
      • Orange2nd · 1,000 pts
      • Lemon3rd · 2,000 pts
      • Grapes4th · 4,000 pts
      • Watermelon5th+ · 8,000 pts

      Difficulty

      Pick Easy, Medium or Hard before you start: use the Left and Right arrow keys on the title screen, or Options › Difficulty. A change made in the middle of a game starts with your next new game. In an online game the host's choice counts.

      EasyBonus items about every 9 seconds, 1.5× as many canes, treasure boxes and power items, a quarter fewer critters on each board (never fewer than 3), and 40% more time before HURRY UP.
      MediumThe standard game: bonus items about every 14 seconds and HURRY UP after 34 seconds.
      HardBonus items only about every 26 seconds, half as many canes, boxes and power items, about 30% more critters on each board, and HURRY UP 30% sooner.

      Special rounds always have the critters they were designed with.

      Bonus items

      Bonus items keep appearing on ledges during a round, each for 10 seconds. How often depends on the difficulty. They're basic items worth points, and can show up on any board: one of 62 foods (100 to 1,000 pts), a cake, a crown or a ruby. Some boards also get one special-power item (see below).

      • Cake2,000 pts
      • Crown5,000 pts
      • Ruby1,000 pts

      Special-power items

      A magic cane turns up on 1 board in 5 and a treasure box on about 1 in 7, but never both on the same board. Separately, about 1 board in 7 gets a potion, umbrella or bubble gum, with or without a cane or box. When a board has one, it appears on a ledge once, 5 to 17 seconds into the round, for 10 seconds. Fruit and the other basic items can show up on any board.

      • Magic Cane

        When the round ends, every bubble still floating turns into a surprise prize, and a giant one a third of the screen wide drops to the bottom. The prize is usually a food (strawberry, peach, banana, pepper, cake, shaved ice or grapes), sometimes a diamond. The giant food is worth 10,000 (strawberry, peach), 20,000 (banana, pepper) or 30,000 (cake, shaved ice, grapes). Each small one is worth a tenth of its giant. Leave plenty of bubbles floating!

      • Treasure Box

        Comes in five colors. When the round ends, every bubble still floating turns into a diamond in the box's color, and a giant diamond in the same color drops to the bottom. A giant gold diamond is worth 70,000, red 60,000, magenta 50,000, blue 40,000 and green 30,000. Each small one is worth a tenth of its giant (gold 7,000 down to green 3,000).

      • Potion

        Every critter vanishes and clovers fill the top half of the screen. Grab as many as you can in 15 seconds. Getting them all adds up to 40,000 pts, enough for two extra dinos.

      • Umbrella

        Every critter vanishes and you warp ahead, skipping the next 3 rounds (round 5 takes you to round 9).

      • Bubble Gum

        Comes in pink, blue or yellow wrappers, and every color gives the power. Your bubbles shoot faster and farther, you can blow more than twice as fast, and up to 15 can be on screen at once. A gum icon on your side wall shows it's on. It lasts through later rounds until you lose a life. In 2 player, only the dino who grabbed it gets the power.

      Zones

      Every 5 rounds a new zone starts and something new shows up. After that it gets mixed into later rounds now and then (not every round), and the round intro tells you what this round has in store. When something new shows up (a new critter, twist or special round), the intro stays up for 7 seconds so you can read it. Prefer a cleaner screen? Untick Options › Captions & descriptions to hide the pop-up comments (like FROZEN! or GLUG GLUG GLUG), the round names and descriptions, and the twist name by the round number. Points, READY!, HURRY UP! and warnings still show, and rounds start right away. From round 21 the plain layouts are gone: levels get more critters and wilder shapes, and about 1 round in 6 borrows a gadget from the special rounds (a pair of portals, a conveyor belt or two, or a bumper). Critters also get a little faster every round.

      Special rounds

      Rounds 3, 8, 13 … 58 and every 10th round are specially designed levels with their own twist. The round intro tells you what to do, and the twist's name shows next to the round number.

      Round 3Pinball Palace: three bumpers knock you (and your bubbles) flying. Hit all three within 4 seconds for a 10,000 point JACKPOT.
      Round 8Conveyor Factory: the platforms are belts that carry Bub and Bob along (critters walk on them normally). Touch the red switch to reverse them all.
      Round 10Fossil Grin: a giant dino skull with critters in its eyes. The teeth snap shut every 8 seconds.
      Round 13Portal Rooms: four sealed rooms joined by matching portals. The gold portal in the middle sends you somewhere random.
      Round 18Mirror Match: a glass wall splits the level. Playing alone, your shadow twin copies you on the other side, popping bubbles and grabbing treats. Keep it away from critters too!
      Round 20Name in Lights: the platforms spell BUB. Pop a critter standing on each letter to light it; light all three for a star shower.
      Round 23Slither: a snake made of platform tiles crawls round the level and grows. When its head reaches its tail it bites, and every critter is knocked out.
      Round 28Bubble Bath: the tub keeps filling. Underwater you swim (tap jump) and blow bubbles faster, but critters swim fast too. Pop the plug bubble to drain it.
      Round 30Rainbow Bridge: your bubbles take the color of the arch you stand on. Pop 3 of one color together for a rainbow blast that knocks out every critter.
      Round 33Switchback: pop the arrow bubble to flip gravity for everyone. Critters walk on the ceilings too.
      Round 38Treasure Vault: collect 3 keys (two up top, one in a bubble) to open the vault for a gem rush.
      Round 40Saucer Raid: the whole level is a flying saucer that slides and sinks every 10 seconds. When it touches down, HURRY UP!
      Round 43Storm Manor: a dark haunted house. Ghosts are invisible except when the lightning flashes.
      Round 48Fault Line: a cracked cavern. A rumble warns you, then every 8 seconds a big quake shakes everything and leaves the walking critters dizzy (stars spin round their heads) for a few seconds. They can't move or shoot while dizzy, so trap them fast.
      Round 50Eruption: every 15 seconds the volcano throws out rocks and the lava rises a level. Lava knocks you out.
      Round 53Thunder Tower: every critter wears a metal helmet, so bubbles just bounce off. Lightning bubbles wait by the walls on each floor (and come back a few seconds after you use one). Pop one when the critters are on that floor to zap them all.
      Round 58Flood Gates: the critters are red-hot and burst any bubble that touches them. Pop the water bubble at the top: the flood zigzags down every floor and washes away every critter in its path. It comes back after each flood.
      Round 60Whirlpool: a spiral with a water bubble waiting in the middle. Get there first and flush everything out.
      Round 70Clockwork: the clock's hands are platforms that tick round. Pop critters while the long hand points to 12 for a time bonus.
      Round 80Broken Heart: on the left half you fall up! Trap the two critters with pink rings to mend the heart and finish the round.
      Round 90Frost Keep: an icy castle with the lights out. Your bubbles glow, so blow them to see where you're going.

      Tips

      Extra dino every 20,000 points. Clear a round in under 25 seconds for a 2,000 point bonus. If a round takes longer than 34 seconds (a little sooner every 5 rounds), a HURRY UP banner flashes, the music speeds up and every critter speeds up. Eight seconds later Skel floats in through the walls and hunts the nearest player. Losing a life restarts that clock. After the last critter goes, you get 5 seconds to grab whatever is left, and any bubbles still floating pop. Now and then (about 1 round in 5) every one of them turns into the same snack instead. Every 5 rounds things get crazier: wilder layouts, more critters, tougher kinds, a little more speed, and from round 31 some critters start out angry. Clear all 120 and the game starts over, faster.

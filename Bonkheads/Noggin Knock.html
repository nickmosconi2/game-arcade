      Noggin Knock

      Critters have moved into 24 worlds of platforms. Jump up and bonk the platform under a critter to flip it over, then run into it to kick it off the screen before it wakes up. Clear every critter to finish a level. There are 144 levels, and every sixth one has a boss.

      How to play

      Critters drop in from the top corners and walk the platforms. Jump into the underside of the platform a critter is standing on and it flips over for a few seconds. Touch a flipped critter to kick it away for points, and do it quickly: each kick in a row is worth more. If a flipped critter is left too long it gets up angrier and faster. Bonking a flipped critter again flips it back onto its feet.

      Touching a critter that's awake costs you a noggin (a life). The screen wraps around: walk off one side to come back on the other, and anything that falls off the bottom drops back in at the top. Critters that wrap round come back angrier.

      The THUMP block flips every critter standing on a platform at once. Bonk it from below. It has three uses. Take too long and HURRY UP! appears, and the critters speed up.

          Items

          Kicked critters drop coins. Items fall in from the top now and then.

            Waypoints

            After every six levels you reach a waypoint. Your progress is saved there, so you can pick up from that level later, and you can spend coins on an extra noggin, speed boots or a helmet.

            Difficulty and captions

            Options › Difficulty: Easy gives 5 noggins, slower critters and longer naps. Hard means faster critters, more of them and shorter naps. The game always starts on Medium with captions off. Turn on Options › Captions & descriptions to see a hint when a new critter or boss turns up.

            Playing online

            Choose Game › Play Online… (or press O). Click a friend, or anyone in Everyone online, to invite them or join their game. The host's computer runs the game, so it plays the same for both of you.

            4-player preview

            Press 4 on the title screen to see what a four-player game looks like. Players 1 and 2 play as usual, and players 3 and 4 (Pip and Fern) are computer buddies who bonk and kick alongside you.

            Noggin Knock's 144 level layouts and rules are modelled on the 1998 game Bonkheads by 1AM Entertainment. All the characters, art, names and sounds here are new.

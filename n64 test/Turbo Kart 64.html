      Turbo Kart 64

      Eight racers, three laps around Sunny Raceway. Pick up items from the floating boxes, drift through the bends for a turbo boost, and get to the line first. Race alone against the computer, with a friend on a split screen, or online with up to three friends (the empty seats are filled by computer racers).

      Racing tips

      Rocket start: press accelerate just as the "1" disappears, right before GO, and you shoot off the line.

      Drift turbo: hold the drift button while steering into a bend. Sparks fly from your wheels: blue after a moment, orange if you hold it longer. Let go to get a small (blue) or big (orange) burst of speed.

      Grass slows you right down, unless you're boosting or have a star.

      Items

        Racers near the front mostly get bananas and green shells; racers near the back get the good stuff.

        Classes

        50cc is gentle, 100cc is the normal race and 150cc is fast with sharper computer racers. Pick a class on the title screen (Left and Right) or in Options. Your best race and lap times for each class are kept on this computer.

        Playing online

        Choose Game › Play Online… (or press O). One player presses Host a game; friends join from their list, by typing the host's code, or from an invite. When everyone's in, the host presses Start game. Up to 4 people can race; computer racers fill the other seats. If your copies of the game are different versions, the newer one is passed to the older one automatically: save it over your old copy and open it again.

        Turbo Kart 64 is a personal tribute to 1990s kart racers. The karts, track, music and sound were all made for this game.

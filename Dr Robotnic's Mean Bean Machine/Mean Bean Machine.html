      Dr. Robotnik's Mean Bean Machine

      Dr. Robotnik is rounding up the happy beans of Beanville to turn them into robot slaves in his Mean Bean-Steaming Machine. Beat his henchbots at their own game, all thirteen of them, then take on the Doctor himself.

      Game controllers work too: the stick or d-pad moves and drops, A turns clockwise, B anticlockwise, Start pauses. On a phone, use the buttons under the game.

      How to play

      Beans drop into your well in pairs. Line up four or more of the same colour (side by side or on top of each other) and they pop. Beans above fall down, and if that lines up another group, it pops too: that's a chain, and chains are worth far more.

      Every pop sends refugee beans into your opponent's well. They show up as faces along the top of the well before they fall. Refugee beans don't join up with anything; they only go when a group pops right next to them. If you pop beans while refugees are on their way to you, you cancel them out first.

      Your well fills up from the bottom. When the beans reach the top of the third column, where new pairs come in, you lose. Squeeze the pair between two walls and press turn twice quickly to flip it over.

      While a chain is going, your score shows what it's worth: points × multiplier, for example 50×18.

      Modes

      Scenario: thirteen stages against Robotnik's henchbots, each a little sharper than the last, ending with the Doctor in his lab. Win and you get a time bonus; lose and you can continue from the same stage. Continue picks up from the furthest stage you've reached on this computer.

      1P vs 2P: two players on one keyboard (or online). Each picks a level from 1 to 5 (how fast the beans fall), and the screen keeps count of the games won.

      Exercise: on your own. Every 25 pairs the level goes up, the beans fall faster, and refugee beans start dropping in now and then. How long can you last?

          Difficulty and captions

          Options › Difficulty: Easy plays with four colours instead of five and gives you slower, clumsier opponents; Hard makes them quicker and sharper. The game always starts on Medium with captions off. Turn on Options › Captions & descriptions to see what each opponent says before the match.

          Playing online

          Choose Game › Play Online… (or press O). Click a friend, or anyone in Everyone online, to invite them or join their game. The host plays the left-hand well and the friend the right; the host's computer runs the game, so it plays the same for both of you.
